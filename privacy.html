Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Brilliant Unicorn Ltd (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with when you use our solar lead-matching service or fintech consulting services.

1. What we collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide:

  • Full name and contact details (phone, WhatsApp, email)
  • Location (town, estate, or county)
  • Average monthly electricity bill
  • Roof size and property type, if provided
  • Any additional notes you choose to share

We do not collect payment information, ID documents, or biometric data.

2. How we use your information

  • To qualify your site and assess solar feasibility
  • To match you with a vetted installer in your area
  • To follow up with questions or clarifications
  • To improve our matching process over time

3. Who we share it with

We share your details only with one vetted solar installer at a time — the one best suited to your location and system size. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with marketing lists, advertisers, or unrelated third parties.

If you request a second opinion, we may share your details with a second installer, but only with your explicit consent.

4. How long we keep it

We retain lead information for up to 24 months after your last interaction with us, unless you request deletion earlier. After that period, personal identifiers are removed or the record is deleted entirely.

5. Your rights

You may request to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for future sharing

To exercise any of these rights, email ceo@brilliantunicorn.com.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for functionality. We do not use third-party tracking pixels, retargeting ads, or behavioural analytics platforms.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted form submission via Formspree and access controls on our internal records. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted here with the updated date.
